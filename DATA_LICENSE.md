# UniProt data attribution

Protein sequences and annotations in this repository were obtained from
UniProtKB/Swiss-Prot, the reviewed component of UniProtKB, recorded in the project
as release **2026_03**.

UniProt applies the **Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0)**
license to the copyrightable parts of its databases:

- Source: https://www.uniprot.org/
- UniProt licensing: https://www.uniprot.org/help/license
- License terms: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

The data have been modified by filtering entries, reducing EC labels to three
levels, deduplicating sequences within phyla, and assigning experimental splits.
The source-derived sequences and annotations retain their UniProt attribution
and applicable CC BY 4.0 terms. This notice does not assign a license to the
project's original code or manuscript.
